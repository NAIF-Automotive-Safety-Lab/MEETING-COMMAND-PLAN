# POST-MEETING EVIDENCE CONVERSION PROCEDURE

1. Freeze the meeting record.
2. Separate statements into: Project Fact / External Statement / Proposal / Unknown.
3. Create one record for each external technical claim.
4. Attach source: minutes, email, approved attachment, or formal quotation.
5. Convert each accepted statement to:
   Technical Scope → Required Evidence → Entry Criteria → Commercial Structure → NDA Trigger → Engineering Action → Deliverable → Acceptance → Evidence → Owner → Due Date.
6. Mark any unverified statement **PENDING HUMAN RESPONSE**.
7. Do not promote company interest into partnership status.
8. Do not promote commercial discussion into a commitment.
9. Do not promote an engineering suggestion into a V7 change.
10. Route any deeper disclosure through Peter's gate before release.
