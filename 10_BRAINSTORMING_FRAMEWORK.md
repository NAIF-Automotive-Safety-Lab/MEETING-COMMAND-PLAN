# BRAINSTORMING FRAMEWORK

### Classify every idea
A — Compatible Improvement
B — Architecture Modification
C — Design-Intent Change
D — IP / Disclosure Conflict

### Convert
Idea → Technical Impact → Evidence → Decision → Owner → Deadline

### Rules
- No idea becomes authoritative by discussion.
- Geometry edits are working-copy proposals only.
- Any C/D item is escalated for authority review before external disclosure.
- Every idea records its evidence basis or UNKNOWN.
