# ADVERSARIAL / RED-TEAM QUESTIONS

1. What would falsify the architecture?
2. Which current statement is a design hypothesis rather than a measured result?
3. What part of the system is most likely to create an unacceptable interface conflict?
4. What happens if the seat moves but the restraint geometry does not accommodate the changed trajectory?
5. What happens if left/right load sharing is asymmetric?
6. What prevents a kinematic solution from becoming a real mechanism with jam/end-stop/rebound problems?
7. What evidence proves the seat is structurally adequate rather than merely geometrically connected?
8. What vehicle data are still missing?
9. What would make this concept not worth further testing?
10. What is the first test that could kill the hypothesis cheaply?
11. What exactly is the Phase-1 deliverable, and what is explicitly out of scope?
12. Which claims cannot be supported without your organization's own evidence?
13. What data would you refuse to accept as an engineering input because provenance is inadequate?
14. What would prevent a proposed CAD change from becoming an authoritative V7 change?
15. What is the minimum evidence needed before the project should spend money on full vehicle work?

**Response rule:** every answer must be converted to an evidence statement or an explicit unknown. Do not defend the concept by rhetoric.
