# MASTER TECHNICAL QUALIFICATION QUESTION SET
## T-OCS V7.0 REVISION-2

**Instrument type:** Technical Qualification Instrument — not a sales presentation.  
**Rule:** Questions extract what the organization actually offers, requires, accepts, or rejects. No response is inferred.

## C1 — TECHNICAL SCOPE

1. Which of the following services can your organization provide for this class of work?
   Engineering / CAE / FE / CAD / Prototype / Testing / Manufacturing / Co-development
2. Which activities would be performed directly by your organization versus subcontractors?
3. What concrete deliverables would your team propose?
4. What technical disciplines would be assigned?
5. What artifacts would you regard as the completion evidence for each deliverable?

## C2 — REQUIRED EVIDENCE

6. What technical information must be available before your organization can evaluate the concept?
7. Which data are mandatory at project entry, and which can be developed during Phase-1?
8. What engineering evidence is required before you will model, build, or test an article?
9. What acceptance criteria or review gates do you normally require?
10. What evidence do you require to distinguish design intent from validated engineering behavior?

## C3 — ENTRY CRITERIA

11. What are your formal conditions for starting Phase-1?
12. Is an NDA required? At what point?
13. Do you require a defined data package, test article, model definition, requirements baseline, internal approval, or commercial authorization?
14. What configuration / revision controls must exist before technical work starts?
15. What unresolved items can remain open at project entry, and how must they be recorded?

## C4 — COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE

16. What commercial structures are available?
   Paid Engineering / Deferred Engineering / Co-development / Sponsorship / Investment
17. Which structure is actually offered for this type of engagement?
18. What is the deliverable-based pricing basis?
19. What is fixed-price versus variable-price?
20. What constitutes acceptance of the purchased work?
21. What change-control mechanism applies if scope changes?

## C5 — NDA TRIGGER

22. What can be reviewed at L1?
23. What specifically triggers an NDA?
24. What information remains restricted even after NDA?
25. Can your organization receive CAD, detailed mechanism information, test data, or patent-sensitive information under controlled disclosure?
26. What retention, access, subcontracting, publication, portfolio, training, or third-party-use restrictions apply?

## Mandatory Meeting Closeout

Before the meeting is closed, record:

- Meeting Result
- Technical Scope
- Required Evidence
- Entry Criteria
- Commercial Structure
- NDA Trigger
- Engineering Action
- Deliverable
- Acceptance Criterion
- Evidence Requirement
- Owner
- Due Date

Do not close with “interested”, “good meeting”, or “potential partner” without converting the statement into the fields above.
