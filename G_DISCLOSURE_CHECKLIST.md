# DISCLOSURE CHECKLIST — T-OCS V7.0 REVISION-2

## L1 — MAY DISCLOSE
- High-level engineering problem.
- High-level functional architecture.
- Development path.
- Current engineering-stage description.
- Intended validation path.
- Proposed collaboration ask.
- Statements explicitly marked as research/concept/reconstruction status.
- Organization/contact identity supplied in the approved outreach record.

## NDA — MAY BECOME ELIGIBLE FOR CONTROLLED DISCLOSURE AFTER HUMAN DECISION
- Controlled technical details needed to evaluate a scoped Phase-1.
- Detailed engineering requirements requested by the recipient and approved by project authority.
- Controlled technical artifacts only after the NDA trigger is satisfied.

## REMAINS RESTRICTED / NOT FOR L1
- Proprietary mechanism.
- Exact dimensions.
- CAD / STEP.
- Patent-sensitive technical details.
- Detailed force / energy assumptions.
- Internal audit / gap files.
- Internal engineering completion work order.
- Any unvalidated test, CAE, crash, safety, regulatory, manufacturing or customer claim.

## EVIDENCE FIREWALL
Never state as current fact unless documented:
- test results
- simulation results
- manufacturing readiness
- safety compliance
- customer acceptance
- sponsorship
- investment
- partnership
- regulatory approval

Use: **UNKNOWN / OPEN / NOT ESTABLISHED / PENDING HUMAN RESPONSE**

## REQUIRED FINAL CHECK
[ ] L1 contains no prohibited technical detail  
[ ] No exact dimensions disclosed  
[ ] No CAD/STEP disclosed  
[ ] No internal gap/audit package disclosed  
[ ] No unsupported performance claim  
[ ] No unsupported company commitment  
[ ] All material engineering claims have source/reference or UNKNOWN/OPEN status  
[ ] NDA trigger is explicitly decided before controlled disclosure
