# BRAINSTORMING GOVERNANCE

Every new idea is classified immediately:

**A — Compatible Improvement**  
Fits current Design Intent and disclosure boundary. May be proposed without changing the architecture.

**B — Architecture Modification**  
Changes topology or functional relationships. Requires evidence and authority review.

**C — Design-Intent Change**  
Changes a protected intent or requirement. Not an informal meeting change.

**D — IP / Disclosure Conflict**  
Would expose restricted information or create an IP/disclosure issue. Stop and escalate.
